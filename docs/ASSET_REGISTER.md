## Original MyShop assets (authored in this project)

Generated by `scripts/authoring/*` and saved to `assets/models/authored/` and `assets/materials/`. Blender-editable glTF exports are in `assets/models/authored/blender_source/`.

- Harbor Commute body: shell, hood, greenhouse, lights, mirrors, door seams, interior, underbody, alloy rims, brake discs and calipers, and engine-bay sheet metal.
- Engine-bay parts: oil filter, drain plug, oil fill cap, dipstick, air box and intake, coolant/brake/washer reservoirs, hoses, cables, wiring loom.
- Two-post lift, oil drain caddy, placement ghost, workbench, pegboard, sectional door, wall pilasters, floor drain.
- All materials in `assets/materials/`.

| `ccs_hellcat.glb`, `ccs_gt3rs.glb`, `bmw+suv+3d+model.glb` (Downloads) | Real branded vehicles with unknown source. Keep out of the project. |
| Ripped game models, manufacturer CAD, real badges or names | Trademark and copyright risk. See the vehicle design rule in `PRODUCT_VISION.md`. |
| Tripo-generated models | Not used. Check Tripo's terms, scale and cleanup before any import. |
